Your first visit

What happens
when you come in

A private clinic in a home setting — unhurried, thorough, and grounded in classical Chinese medicine. Here's what to expect from start to finish.

01
You text or email to book

Send a text to (416) 871-9239 with your name, what you're coming in for, and whether you'd prefer in-person or virtual. We'll reply with available times within 24 hours. No online booking system — just a direct conversation.

02
Short intake form before you arrive

We'll send a brief intake form covering your health history and chief concern. Completing it in advance means your full appointment time is spent on your clinical picture, not paperwork.

03
Constitutional assessment

The first session includes a detailed intake: pulse reading, tongue diagnosis, and a conversation about what's been happening in your body. You leave with a clear picture of what we're treating and why.

04
Treatment — on the same visit

Acupuncture or a custom herbal formula, depending on what your clinical picture calls for. You don't wait for a second appointment to begin treatment.

05
A written plan you leave with

After your first visit you'll have a written summary of your constitutional pattern and a care plan with a clear arc — beginning, middle, and end. Not an open-ended treatment series.

In-person sessions at our home clinic in Mono, Ontario. Virtual consultations available across Canada by arrangement. Two days per week, by appointment only. New patients accepted by direct enquiry.
