The framework behind every consultation

What is the Quantum
Elemental Model™?

Most treatment approaches ask: what condition does this person have? The Quantum Elemental Model™ asks a different question: what kind of person has this condition?

QEM™ is a constitutional framework developed by Vu Le that integrates classical Five Element theory with BaZi natal chart analysis. It produces a clinical picture that is specific to your elemental type, your current elemental cycle, and the seasonal pressures active right now — not a generic protocol applied to a diagnosis.

The result is treatment that is built around you. Your constitution determines which organ systems are under load. Your elemental cycle determines which interventions will work right now. Your pattern — not your label — determines the formula.

Read the full framework explanation →

Why the same condition presents differently

IBS in a Wood constitutional type is a Liver-Spleen disharmony pattern. In an Earth type it is Spleen Qi deficiency with dampness. Same diagnosis — completely different root — different formula. QEM™ identifies which root applies to you.

Why the same treatment doesn't work for everyone

A herb that strengthens one constitutional type may overwhelm another. Timing matters — your ten-year elemental cycle determines which organs are under pressure right now, and which interventions are appropriate in this particular window.

What it means in practice

Every consultation begins with a constitutional assessment before any treatment is chosen. The pattern comes first. The formula, acupuncture protocol, and dietary guidance follow from the pattern — specific to you, not to your diagnosis.
