Scope of practice

What this practice
does and does not do.

This is not a medical diagnosis

TCM practitioners do not diagnose or treat medical conditions as defined under the Medicine Act. What we offer is a constitutional assessment expressed in classical Chinese medical language.

We do not replace your physician

This practice is complementary, not a substitute for primary care. We encourage you to maintain your relationship with your physician and are happy to work alongside your healthcare team.

Herbal prescriptions are individualised

Disclose all current medications and supplements at intake so herb-drug interactions can be properly assessed. Withholding health information may affect safety and effectiveness.

Results vary and timelines are honest

Constitutional medicine works cumulatively. No outcome is guaranteed. What we commit to is a rigorous, pattern-informed clinical approach at every contact.

Emergency situations

This practice is not equipped to manage acute emergencies. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department.

Regulatory standing

Vu Le is registered with the CTCMPAO and holds NCBAHM Diplomate credentials. In good standing.

Informed consent

What you’re agreeing to
when we work together.

Nature of treatment. Services by Vu Le (R.TCMP, R.Ac, NCBAHM Diplomate) are within the regulated scope of Traditional Chinese Medicine under the Regulated Health Professions Act (Ontario). Services include virtual constitutional assessment, Chinese herbal medicine prescription, and clinical follow-up. Acupuncture, cupping, moxibustion, and physical modalities are in-person only.

Risks and benefits. Chinese herbal medicine carries risks including adverse reactions and herb-drug interactions. You will disclose your full health history — including all medications, supplements, allergies, and medical conditions — at intake and at each subsequent contact.

Voluntary participation. Participation is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw consent or decline any recommendation at any time without penalty.

Not a substitute for medical care. TCM does not replace primary medical care. You will continue to consult your physician for conditions within the scope of medicine.

Confidentiality. Your health information is private and treated with the same confidentiality obligations that apply to all regulated healthcare practitioners in Ontario.

Communication. This practice communicates by text. By initiating contact at (416) 871-9239, you consent to receive clinical follow-up by SMS. You may withdraw at any time.

Payment. Payment is required before service. Interac e-Transfer to lemnvu@outlook.com. Formulas are not dispatched until payment is confirmed.
