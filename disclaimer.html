Scope of practice

What this practice
does and does not do.

The Quantum Elemental Model™ framework operates entirely within the regulated scope of Traditional Chinese Medicine as defined by the CTCMPAO (Ontario). The following points help set clear expectations before your first contact.

◎
This is not a medical diagnosis

TCM practitioners do not diagnose, treat, or manage medical conditions as defined under the Medicine Act. What we offer is a constitutional assessment — a pattern-based picture of your body’s current state — expressed in classical Chinese medical language, not Western medical terminology.

◎
We do not replace your physician

This practice is complementary, not a substitute for primary care. If you have an active medical condition or are under the care of a physician, we encourage you to maintain that relationship. We are happy to work alongside your existing healthcare team.

◎
Herbal prescriptions are individualised

Chinese herbal formulas prescribed through this practice are customised to your constitutional pattern. It is important that you disclose all current medications and supplements at intake so that herb-drug interactions can be properly assessed.

◎
Results vary and timelines are honest

Constitutional medicine works cumulatively. Some patients notice shifts within the first week; for others, meaningful change emerges over several months. No outcome is guaranteed. What we do commit to is a rigorous, pattern-informed clinical approach at every contact.

◎
Emergency situations

This practice is not equipped to manage acute emergencies. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department. Do not contact this practice in lieu of emergency services.

◎
Regulatory standing

Vu Le is registered with the College of Traditional Chinese Medicine Practitioners and Acupuncturists of Ontario (CTCMPAO) and holds NCBAHM Diplomate credentials. In good standing.

Informed consent

What you’re agreeing to
when we work together.

By contacting this practice and proceeding with consultation, you acknowledge the following.

Nature of treatment. The services provided by Vu Le (R.TCMP, R.Ac, NCBAHM Diplomate) are within the regulated scope of Traditional Chinese Medicine as defined by the Regulated Health Professions Act (Ontario). Services include virtual constitutional assessment, Chinese herbal medicine prescription, and clinical follow-up. Acupuncture, cupping, moxibustion, and physical treatment modalities are available in-person only and are not provided through virtual consultations.

Risks and benefits. Chinese herbal medicine carries risks which may include adverse reactions, herb-drug interactions, and temporary aggravation of symptoms. You will disclose your full health history — including all medications, supplements, allergies, and relevant medical conditions — at intake and at each subsequent contact. Withholding relevant health information may affect the safety and effectiveness of treatment.

Voluntary participation. Participation in treatment is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw consent for any treatment or decline any recommendation at any time without penalty. You are entitled to ask questions about your treatment plan and to receive clear, honest answers.

Not a substitute for medical care. TCM does not replace primary medical care. You will continue to consult your physician for conditions that fall within the scope of medicine, and will inform your practitioner of any changes to your medical situation.

Confidentiality. Your health information is private and is treated with the same confidentiality obligations that apply to all regulated healthcare practitioners in Ontario. Records are not shared without express written consent, except as required by law.

Communication. This practice communicates by text. By initiating contact at (416) 871-9239, you consent to receive clinical follow-up and practice communications by SMS. You may withdraw at any time.

Virtual consultation & jurisdiction

Where we can
serve you virtually.

Virtual TCM consultations are conducted under CTCMPAO telepractice guidelines (May 2021 position statement). Vu Le is licensed in Ontario and provides virtual services to patients in provinces and territories where TCM is unregulated at the provincial level, or where there is no conflicting regulatory requirement.

Ontario Saskatchewan Manitoba Nova Scotia New Brunswick Prince Edward Island Yukon Northwest Territories Nunavut
Virtual consultations are not available to patients currently residing in British Columbia, Alberta, Quebec, or Newfoundland & Labrador, as those provinces have their own TCM regulatory colleges. Patients in those provinces should seek a practitioner registered with their provincial college.
