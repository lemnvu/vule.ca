Methods & frameworks

Twenty years of
classical practice.

The same constitutional intelligence that governs the body governs the land. Vu Le has conducted audits for private residences, family offices, commercial developments, and land acquisitions across Asia, the Middle East, the Caribbean, and North America.

Flying Stars — Xuan Kong Fei Xing
Time & Space Analysis

The forces active in a property across its natal chart and current period. Time-based assessment of what is working in a space and what is working against it — specific to the facing direction, construction date, and current 20-year cycle.

Eight Mansions — Ba Zhai
Personal Destiny Mapping

Alignment between the individual and the space they occupy. Understanding which sectors and directions serve each person’s constitution and goals — and which create friction, depletion, or conflict.

BaZi — Destiny Analysis
Strategic Timing

For major life and business decisions. Timing, direction, and the hidden constitutional forces at work across a person’s life chart. The elemental fingerprint that shapes decision, health, and opportunity.

Form School & San He
Environmental Qi

Landform and environmental qi. The foundation beneath every built environment — what the land itself is doing before a building is placed on it. Applicable to acquisition decisions and site selection.

Qi Men Dun Jia
Strategic Selection

Strategic timing and directional selection for significant decisions and transitions. Used alongside BaZi for high-stakes choices where the window of action matters as much as the direction of action.

Yin Feng Shui
Ancestral Consultation

Ancestral site consultation. The unseen inheritance that shapes the living — for families and lineages who understand that the past has a geography. Conducted with appropriate discretion and protocol.

A building is never neutral. The same forces that govern constitutional health in the body govern the energetic condition of a space. Classical Feng Shui reads both — the person and the environment — in relation to each other.

Engagements

Who this work
is for.

Classical Feng Shui is not a decorating service. The clients who find this work most valuable are those who understand that environment is not passive — that the orientation of a bedroom, the facing direction of an office, the landform surrounding a site are not merely aesthetic variables but active forces shaping energy, health, decision-making, and outcomes.

Engagements are considered individually. There is no standard package. The appropriate scope — audit type, depth of analysis, number of visits — depends on the property, the client’s situation, and the purpose of the consultation.

All enquiries are handled directly and confidentially. Text a brief description of your property and purpose and we will be in touch within 24 hours.

Residential audit — existing home or pre-purchase due diligence

How an engagement works

From enquiry
to audit.

01
Initial enquiry
Text (416) 871-9239 with a brief description of your property, your situation, and what you’re hoping to understand. Include the address if available, or a general description of the property type and location.
02
Preliminary assessment
Based on your description, we will confirm whether the engagement is appropriate, outline the scope of work, and provide a fee estimate. For most residential audits this happens within 24–48 hours of initial contact.
03
Data collection
For Feng Shui audits: floor plan, facing direction compass reading, and construction or renovation date. For BaZi: date, time, and location of birth. Remote audits are conducted using satellite imagery and submitted floor plans where an in-person visit is not feasible.
04
Analysis & report
The natal chart of the property is plotted and current period overlays applied. Analysis is delivered in a written report with specific sector-by-sector findings and actionable recommendations — not generic advice, but specific to this property in this period for these occupants.
05
Debrief & questions
A follow-up call or text exchange to walk through findings, clarify recommendations, and answer questions. Annual review available for ongoing clients as elemental cycles shift.
