How an engagement works

From enquiry
to audit.

01
Initial enquiry

Text (416) 871-9239 with a brief description of your property, your situation, and what you’re hoping to understand.

02
Preliminary assessment

We confirm whether the engagement is appropriate, outline the scope of work, and provide a fee estimate — usually within 24–48 hours.

03
Data collection

Floor plan, facing direction compass reading, and construction date. For BaZi: date, time, and location of birth. Remote audits conducted using satellite imagery and submitted floor plans.

04
Analysis & report

The natal chart of the property is plotted and current period overlays applied. Written report with specific sector-by-sector findings and actionable recommendations.

05
Debrief & questions

A follow-up call or text exchange to walk through findings, clarify recommendations, and answer questions. Annual review available as elemental cycles shift.
