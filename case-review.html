How the session is structured

Sixty minutes.
A diagnosis first.

Most consultations begin with the patient explaining their case and the practitioner listening. This session is structured differently — I present my constitutional findings first, based on your intake and QEM™ analysis, and you respond to what you hear. This is deliberate. It demonstrates what a different framework produces before we discuss protocol.

01
Pre-session intake

After your initial text and callback, I send a detailed intake form covering your full medical history, previous treatments, and current presentation. Your date of birth is the starting point for QEM™ elemental analysis — completed before we speak.

02
The first twenty minutes — diagnosis

I present my constitutional findings: your elemental profile, the environmental and temporal pressures I have identified, and the pattern driving your condition. You hear a diagnosis first. This immediately demonstrates what a different clinical framework produces — and gives you something concrete to respond to.

03
The middle twenty minutes — refinement

You refine the picture. Fill in what the intake didn't capture, correct anything that doesn't fit, add the nuances that never make it into a form. We build the complete assessment together.

04
The final twenty minutes — protocol

What I would do, in what sequence, and a clear explanation of what prior treatment missed and why. Not a vague direction — a specific care pathway with a beginning, a middle, and an expected end point.
