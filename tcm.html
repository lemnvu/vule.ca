Clinical practice

What we do —
and how.

Two classical modalities, one constitutional view. Every visit builds on what came before. Nothing is rushed.

Acupuncture

Fine needles placed at specific points to shift how qi moves through the body's channels. Effective for pain, stress, sleep, hormonal regulation, digestive complaints, and conditions that haven't responded to other approaches. Eligible for most extended health benefit plans.

Herbal Medicine

Classical Chinese herbal formulas, custom-blended for each patient's constitution. Not off-the-shelf supplements — granule medicines prepared and dispensed in-clinic. The first 5-day formula is included in the herbal consultation fee. Same-formula refills can be arranged by text.

Constitutional Diagnosis

Pulse reading, tongue observation, and a careful case history — the classical intake that informs everything else. The QEM™ framework maps your elemental constitution and helps identify the root of patterns that have persisted despite other treatments. First visit is always an assessment.

Extended health benefits & insurance

Acupuncture is eligible for extended health benefits where your plan provides coverage for registered TCM practitioners (R.TCMP / R.Ac).

Herbal medicine is not covered by extended health benefits and is billed directly to the patient.

Insurance disclaimer: It is the patient's sole responsibility to verify coverage with their insurance provider prior to treatment. Not all insurance plans cover services from this practice. We do not guarantee, confirm, or assume responsibility for any insurance reimbursement. All fees are due in full at the time of service regardless of insurance status.
