Overview

One framework.
Many layers.

Classical Chinese medicine has always worked with constitutional types — the recognition that a person’s fundamental nature shapes how disease develops, how they respond to treatment, and what will actually work long-term. QEM™ takes that principle seriously and builds it into a structured clinical method.

The framework integrates classical Five Element theory — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water — with BaZi natal chart analysis to produce a constitutional map specific to each person’s date, time, and place of birth. This elemental fingerprint shapes how you process food, handle stress, which organs are under load, and which seasons tend to destabilise you.

On top of the natal constitution, QEM™ layers current elemental cycle analysis — the ten-year Luck Pillar and the annual elemental energies — to identify which pressures are active now. The result is treatment that is specific to you: constitutional, temporal, and precise.

Why the same condition presents differently

IBS in a Wood constitution is Liver-Spleen disharmony. In an Earth constitution it is Spleen Qi deficiency with dampness. Same diagnosis — different root — different formula.

Why the same treatment doesn’t work for everyone

A herb that strengthens one constitutional type may overwhelm another. Your ten-year elemental cycle determines which organs are under pressure right now.

What it means in practice

Constitutional assessment precedes all treatment decisions. The pattern comes first. The formula and acupuncture protocol follow from the pattern — specific to you.

Common questions

What people ask
before they begin.

Do I need to believe in Chinese medicine for this to work?
No. The constitutional assessment produces a clinical picture regardless of belief. The formulas are pharmacologically active. The element analysis identifies which organ systems are under load. Neither requires belief — they require accurate observation and appropriate treatment selection.
Is virtual assessment as accurate as in-person?
For constitutional and herbal medicine work, yes — with appropriate adaptations. Tongue assessment via photograph is reliable. Constitutional inquiry is unaffected by medium. Pulse is partially adapted. Acupuncture cannot be replicated virtually and is available in-person at two Ontario locations.
Do I need my birth time for the BaZi analysis?
Birth time is preferred and produces a more precise chart. If unavailable, a three-pillar analysis (Year, Month, Day) still provides significant constitutional information. Approximately one third of patients don’t know their birth time — this does not prevent a meaningful assessment.
How long before herbal medicine produces results?
Acute presentations typically respond within five to fourteen days. Chronic conditions usually require two to four months of consistent treatment before the underlying pattern shifts. The trajectory matters more than the speed of initial response.
Can QEM™ be used alongside conventional medical treatment?
Yes. Disclose all medications at intake so herb-drug interactions can be assessed individually. Formulas are adjusted accordingly.
