Extended health benefits & insurance

Acupuncture is HST-exempt and may be eligible for extended health benefits where your plan covers Registered TCM Practitioners (R.TCMP / R.Ac). Coverage varies significantly between plans.

Herbal medicine is subject to HST and is not covered by extended health benefits. All herbal products are billed directly to the patient.

Virtual consultations — check your specific plan for telemedicine or alternative health coverage.
It is the patient's sole responsibility to confirm coverage with their insurance provider before treatment. This clinic does not contact insurers, submit claims, pre-authorize treatment, or guarantee reimbursement. All fees are due in full at time of service regardless of insurance status.
