Why TCM for IBS

IBS has a pattern.
TCM is built to read it.

In classical TCM, what Western medicine calls IBS maps to specific organ system imbalances — Liver overacting on Spleen, Spleen Qi deficiency, Damp-Heat accumulation, Kidney Yang deficiency. These are not metaphors. They are clinical categories that produce predictable, distinct presentations — and respond to different formulas. Understanding which pattern you carry is the entire basis of effective treatment.

The stress-IBS connection

In TCM, the Liver governs the smooth flow of Qi throughout the body — including through the digestive tract. Chronic stress causes Liver Qi stagnation, which directly disrupts digestive function. This is why IBS reliably worsens under stress — not as a psychological phenomenon, but as a physiological one.

Why elimination diets often fail

Elimination diets address triggers, not the constitutional vulnerability that makes those triggers a problem. Two people with identical diets can have completely different digestive function based on their constitutional pattern. Until the pattern is addressed, sensitivity to triggers tends to widen rather than narrow.

The QEM™ advantage

The Quantum Elemental Model™ adds temporal precision to constitutional analysis — your date of birth reveals an elemental profile that predicts which organ systems are under pressure at which times. This explains why IBS flares seasonally or at specific life stages, and produces a more targeted formula than pattern diagnosis alone.

Herbal medicine as the primary tool

For digestive conditions, herbal medicine is often more effective than acupuncture alone — because the formula works continuously between sessions, not just during treatment. The formula is your daily medicine, adjusted as your pattern responds and shifts.

This is not an indefinite treatment relationship. The IBS Resolution Programme has a clear structure with a beginning, a middle, and an expected endpoint. You will know at every stage what we are working toward and why.

Tongue photograph, self-reported BP and heart rate, and a thorough clinical interview. QEM™ natal chart analysis from your date of birth produces your elemental profile. From this, the specific constitutional pattern driving your IBS is identified — not assumed from symptom name alone.

03
First formula — 5-day trial

A custom formula is compounded and shipped. The first five days confirm the formula direction. You report back by text — how your bowels responded, any changes in energy, sleep, or bloating. This feedback is built into the next prescription adjustment.

04
Formula refinement

The formula is adjusted based on your response. This is the most critical phase — the initial formula establishes the direction, and refinement makes it precise. Most cases require one to three adjustments before the formula is settled.

05
Maintenance and consolidation

Once the pattern is responding, refill intervals extend. The goal is resolution that holds without continued formula support — not indefinite dependence on herbal medicine. When that point is reached, we discuss what maintenance looks like, if anything.
